# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

This README is the index and entry point for OctoAcme's project management process documentation. OctoAcme uses a structured, iterative project management lifecycle that moves from initiation and planning through execution, release, and continuous improvement. Projects begin with a one-pager that defines the problem, goals, success metrics, stakeholders, timeline, initial risks, and proposed team, then advance into planning once priorities and capacity are confirmed.

During planning and execution, Project Managers coordinate schedules, risks, dependencies, communication, and delivery routines, while Product Managers define outcomes, prioritize the backlog, and align work to customer and business value. Developers build and test the work, participate in design and code review, and surface technical risks. QA and testing contributors validate acceptance criteria and overall quality, and stakeholders provide input, approvals, and escalation support when trade-offs or business decisions are required.

Day-to-day delivery is tracked through a project board with stages such as Backlog, Ready, In Progress, In Review, QA, and Done, supported by recurring standups, weekly syncs, demos, and stakeholder updates. Risks and dependencies are captured in a risk register, reviewed regularly, and escalated from the team to the Project Manager, Product Lead, and sponsor when delivery or business outcomes are at risk. Quality and release practices are built into the workflow through small pull requests, explicit acceptance criteria, CI checks for tests, linting, and security scanning, manual QA when needed, staging validation, release notes, rollback planning, and retrospectives that turn lessons learned into tracked improvements.
